This is a project i have done on python.
You can type any string or words or sentences and it will count the number of vowels , words and will even reverse the word or 
sentence that you will input!
Very fun. Right?
The main theme of the project is about string.
what is a string?
- it includes words and sentences
- it doesnt include numbers or booleans

what is the projects goal?
- to know how many words i typed in
- to know how many vowels are in the words
- to know the reverse of the word
what are the functions included?
- the count function: it counts the number of words in a sentence
- the vowel function: it counts the number of vowels("a , e, i, o, u")in the word or sentence
- the reverse function: it reverses the words or sentence
it can be used to decode messages like reversed words or sentences
